# Pulse — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Pulse (“the app”) is an interval timer for workouts. It is built to work fully on your device.

## Data we collect

Pulse does **not** collect, sell, or share personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no crash reporting in version 1.0.

## Data stored on your device

The app may store, only locally:

- Workout routines you create
- Session history (routine name, duration, completion)
- Playlists you create and references to audio files you choose
- Settings (language, haptics, music-during-rest behavior)

This data never leaves your phone unless you back up the device with your OS vendor (for example Google Backup), which is outside Pulse.

## Music and files

If you grant audio permission, Pulse reads music files already on the device so you can play them during work intervals. Pulse does not upload those files.

## Notifications

Pulse uses a media playback foreground service so the timer and music can continue when the screen is off. You can disable notifications in system settings.

## Permissions

- Read audio / media library: optional, for local playlists
- Notifications: timer and playback controls
- Foreground service (media playback): keep timer and music running

## Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app privacy text.
